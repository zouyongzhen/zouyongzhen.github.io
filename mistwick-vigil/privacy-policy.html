Mistwick Vigil · Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains what the current Mistwick Vigil app stores on your device, how it selects the initial language, and what happens when you open this remotely hosted page.

Document version 1.1Effective November 23, 2026Applies to the Android app and this website

1. Overview and scope

Mistwick Vigil is a single-player game designed for offline core gameplay. The developer is the person responsible for the app and can be contacted using the email address in the Contact section.

This policy applies to the Mistwick Vigil Android app and the game-specific pages on this website. It describes the current version; it does not cover unrelated apps or websites linked from a store, email application, or your browser.

In short: the app has no account system, advertising, behavioral analytics, or in-app purchases. Gameplay settings and progress are stored locally. We do not operate a gameplay server that receives your save data.

2. Information stored on your device

To restore your experience, the app may store the following information in its local application storage:

  • Your selected interface language, audio levels, sound settings, controls, and display preferences.
  • Your selected watchkeeper and route, unlocked routes or roles, best results, and other supported game progress.
  • The versions or acknowledgement state of in-app disclosures and legal documents, where the app records them.

This information is used only to provide and restore local app functionality. It is not a remote account profile. The current game does not ask you to provide your name, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

3. Initial device language selection

On first launch, the app reads the language preferences supplied by Android to choose a supported interface language. If your preferred language is not supported, the app uses English. Once you manually choose a language, that selection is saved locally and takes priority on later launches.

The language preference is used for interface presentation and is not transmitted to us by the core game.

4. Network use and remote legal pages

Core gameplay does not require an internet connection. The in-app privacy policy and terms links point to pages hosted on GitHub Pages, so those pages need an internet connection and may be unavailable if your device or the hosting service is offline.

When you choose to open a remote legal page, your browser or WebView makes a standard web request to GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information, such as your IP address, requested URL, timestamp, and browser or device headers, to deliver and protect its service. The app does not add your save data to these links.

This website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies set by us. Your browser, network provider, and GitHub Pages have their own data practices.

5. Third-party services and external links

We do not integrate advertising networks, behavioral analytics providers, payment processors, social login services, or a developer-operated gameplay backend in the current app.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and its legal documents. An app store may process information when you browse, download, install, or update the app under its own privacy terms. If you choose an email link, your email application and provider process the message and its contents.

The developer does not sell or rent personal information. No personal information is shared by the core game with the developer or third parties for advertising or behavioral profiling.

6. Retention and deletion

Local settings and progress remain on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Either action deletes the app's locally stored settings, unlocks, results, and progress. Back up anything your device or app store offers separately if you want to keep it; the game itself does not provide cloud save synchronization.

An active watch is not uploaded to a developer server when the app exits. Information processed independently by GitHub, an app store, or your email provider is retained according to that provider's policies.

7. Security

Keeping gameplay data in the app's local storage reduces the need to transmit it. Remote legal pages are served over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device and operating system updated and use the device's available access protections.

8. Children's privacy

The current app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration, advertising, analytics, or a gameplay server. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of remote web links and email where appropriate. If you believe personal information has been sent to us unnecessarily, contact us so we can review and delete it from our own records where required.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can play the core game without creating an account, change the interface language in the app, avoid opening remote pages or email links, and delete local data using Android's controls.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information you have voluntarily sent to us, such as a support email. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information in our own records, subject to legal retention obligations and verification needs. Platform- or GitHub-held information must be addressed through the relevant provider.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, hosting, or data practices change. The document version and effective date at the top identify the current text. Material changes will be presented in the app or on this website where appropriate. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy, except where applicable law requires a different form of notice or consent.
