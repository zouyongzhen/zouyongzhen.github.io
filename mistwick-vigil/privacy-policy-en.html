Mistwick Vigil · Privacy

Privacy policy

Mistwick Vigil is a single-player offline game. This page explains how the current version handles game data on your device.

Version 1.0Updated November 23, 2026Applies to the Android app
On this pageScopeLocal dataPermissions and third partiesRetention and deletionYour choicesPolicy changesContact

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Mistwick Vigil Android app and the privacy information published on this website. Game features, configuration, and saves run or remain on your device in the current version.

When the app starts, it may read the device system language to choose an initial interface language. It does not use that information to build a profile.

2. Local data

To restore your experience, the app may save the following in its Android app data directory.

  • Language, sound, controls, volume, and display settings.
  • Your selected role and route, route unlocks, and best watch results.
  • The versions of disclosures or terms you have acknowledged.

This data is used to restore settings, progress, and results on the same device. The current version does not require an account.

3. Permissions and third parties

The current version is designed for offline play. It has no advertising, behavioral analytics, payments, account system, or server communication. The developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The website may open your device's mail app when you choose an email link. Your email provider handles anything that happens after you send that message.

If a future version adds online features, accounts, or third-party services, the relevant data types, purposes, and choices will be described in the app and on an updated page.

4. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the current device until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Either action removes saved settings, unlocks, and progress.

A watch in progress is not stored in the cloud when the app exits.

5. Your choices

You can choose whether to use the website's email link. You can remove local game data using the Android controls described above. Because the current version does not maintain a remote account or profile, there is no server-side account to access or close.

6. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app's features or data handling change. The version and update date at the top of this page identify the current text. Material changes will be described in the app or on this website where appropriate.
