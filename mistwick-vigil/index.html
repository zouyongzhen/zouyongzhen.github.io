A quiet game of survival

Carry one small light into the whole night.

Choose a watchkeeper, move through the horde, collect experience, and shape each run through blessings and breakthroughs. Every upgrade is a trade-off; the next wave puts it to the test.

◈

Choose your weapon

From blades and spears to hooks, whips, hammers, and meteor hammers, 30 watchkeepers bring different ranges, rhythms, and attack shapes.

☼

Let blessings shape a run

Choose one of three blessings each time you level up. Attack, critical chance, range, movement, and survival pull your build in different directions.

Hold through every dawn

A guard battle arrives every five stages. Defeat every guard on the current route to break through and walk deeper into the mountain.

Settings, unlocks, and progress stay on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes them; they are not synchronized to the cloud.
